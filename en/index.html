Translate on select TRANSLATE

Highlight foreign text and the translation appears right beside it. No buttons, no new tabs. Need it elsewhere? One click to copy.

Long pages, short SUMMARY

Press Ctrl + Shift + V and a full page becomes three sentences and a few keywords. Bring your own AI endpoint — DeepSeek, GLM, any OpenAI-compatible API.

A companion in the corner COMPANION

Drag the cat to any corner of a 3×3 grid around your screen. It bubbles up on schedule: drink water, rest your eyes, stretch. Too chatty? Edit its lines yourself.

It has no server

Settings and your API key live only in your browser; selected text and page content go straight to the endpoint you configured, only when you use a feature. Nothing collected, uploaded, or sold. See the privacy policy.
